# 100 More Common French Verbs (26–125)

Each verb shows its **type** (regular or irregular), the **present tense**, plus the "I" form in the **past (passé composé)** and **future (futur simple)**, and an example sentence. Numbering continues from *25 Most Common French Verbs*.

## Pronunciation key

| Spelling | Sounds like |
|---|---|
| **zh** | the "s" in *measure* (French **j** / soft **g**) |
| **uh** | the "a" in *about* |
| **ew** | French **u**: say "ee" with your lips rounded like "oo" |
| **N** (capital) | nasal vowel: hum the vowel through your nose, don't say the "n" |
| **ahN** | nasal *an / en* (as in *France*) |
| **ohN** | nasal *on* (as in *bon*) |
| **aN** | nasal *in / ain / ien* (as in *vin*) |
| **uhN** | nasal *un* (as in *un*) |
| **r** | French throaty "r", made at the back of the throat |

Tips: final consonants are usually silent (*parlent* = "parl"). **on** ("we/one") always uses the *il/elle* form.

# Lesson 1: Needing, staying and holding

## 26. falloir — to be necessary / must (*fah-lwahr*)

**Type:** Irregular (impersonal: il form only)

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| il faut | eel foh | it is necessary / one must |

- Past: **il a fallu** (eel ah fah-lew) — it was necessary
- Future: **il faudra** (eel foh-drah) — it will be necessary
- Sentence: **Il faut partir maintenant.** (eel foh par-teer maN-tuh-nahN) — *We have to leave now.*
- Tip: falloir is impersonal and only used with il; il faut + infinitive = "you have to / we must".

## 27. laisser — to leave / to let (*leh-say*)

**Type:** Regular (-er, 1st group)

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| je laisse | zhuh les | I leave |
| tu laisses | tew les | you leave |
| il/elle laisse | eel/el les | he/she leaves |
| nous laissons | noo leh-sohN | we leave |
| vous laissez | voo leh-say | you leave |
| ils/elles laissent | eel/el les | they leave |

- Past: **j'ai laissé** (zhay leh-say) — I left / let
- Future: **je laisserai** (zhuh les-ray) — I will leave
- Sentence: **Je laisse mes clés sur la table.** (zhuh les may klay sewr lah tah-bluh) — *I leave my keys on the table.*
- Tip: laisser means leaving something behind; to leave a place is partir or quitter.

## Conjugation patterns

- **Regular -er verbs (1st group)** (rester, regarder, travailler, habiter...): drop *-er* and add *-e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent*. Past participle *-é* (*j'ai regardé*), future = infinitive + *-ai* (*je regarderai*).
- **Regular -ir verbs (2nd group)** (finir, choisir, réussir, réfléchir): drop *-ir* and add *-is, -is, -it, -issons, -issez, -issent*. The *-iss-* appears in the plural (*nous finissons*). Past participle *-i* (*j'ai fini*).
- **Regular -re verbs** (entendre, répondre, rendre, attendre, perdre, descendre, vendre): drop *-re* and add *-s, -s, —, -ons, -ez, -ent* (*il attend* has no ending). Past participle *-u* (*j'ai attendu*); future drops the final *e* (*j'attendrai*).
- **Spelling-change -er verbs** keep regular endings but adjust the stem to keep the sound:
  - *c → ç* and *g → ge* before *o*: *nous commençons*, *nous changeons*, *nous voyageons*.
  - Double consonant: *j'appelle / nous appelons*, *je jette / nous jetons*; future *j'appellerai*, *je jetterai*.
  - *e → è*: *j'achète, je me lève, je me promène* (but *nous achetons*); future *j'achèterai*.
  - *é → è*: *je préfère, j'espère, je répète* (but *nous préférons*); the future keeps *é* (*je préférerai*).
  - *y → i* (optional for -ayer): *j'essaie / j'essaye*, *je paie / je paye*.
- **Irregular families** — learn one, and you know the rest:
  - **partir / sortir / dormir / sentir / servir**: singular drops the last stem consonant (*je dors, je sors, je sens, je sers*), plural keeps it (*nous dormons, nous sortons*).
  - **venir / tenir / devenir / revenir / se souvenir**: *je viens, je tiens ... nous venons, ils viennent*; past *venu / tenu*; future with *-dr-* (*je viendrai, je tiendrai, je me souviendrai*).
  - **prendre family** (prendre, apprendre, comprendre): *j'apprends, nous apprenons, ils apprennent*; past *appris*.
  - **mettre family** (mettre, permettre, promettre): *je promets, nous promettons*; past *promis, permis*.
  - **ouvrir / offrir / découvrir**: present uses -er endings (*j'ouvre, tu offres, il découvre*); past *ouvert, offert, découvert*.
  - Others to learn individually: falloir, pleuvoir (impersonal, *il* only), vivre, écrire, suivre, lire, recevoir, reconnaître, courir, boire, mourir, envoyer, conduire, rire, naître, s'asseoir.
- **Verbs that take être in the passé composé** — remember **DR & MRS VANDERTRAMPP**: **D**evenir, **R**evenir, **M**onter, **R**ester, **S**ortir, **V**enir, **A**ller, **N**aître, **D**escendre, **E**ntrer, **R**entrer, **T**omber, **R**etourner, **A**rriver, **M**ourir, **P**artir, **P**asser.
  - In this guide: rester, arriver, sortir, entrer, devenir, revenir, tomber, monter, rentrer, mourir, naître, descendre (*je suis resté(e)*, *elle est née*).
  - **All pronominal verbs** also use être: *je me suis levé(e)*, *je me suis assis(e)*, *je me suis souvenu(e)*, *nous nous sommes promené(e)s*.
  - With être, the past participle agrees with the subject: *elle est arrivée*, *ils sont sortis*.
  - Monter, descendre, sortir, rentrer and passer use **avoir** when they have a direct object: *j'ai monté les valises* (I carried the suitcases up).
