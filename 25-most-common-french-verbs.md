# 25 Most Common French Verbs

Each verb shows the **present tense**, plus the "I" form in the **past (passé composé)** and **future (futur simple)**, and an example sentence.

## Pronunciation key

| Spelling | Sounds like |
|---|---|
| **zh** | the "s" in *measure* (French **j** / soft **g**) |
| **uh** | the "a" in *about* |
| **ew** | French **u**: say "ee" with your lips rounded like "oo" |
| **N** (capital) | nasal vowel: hum the vowel through your nose, don't say the "n" |
| **ahN** | nasal *an / en* (as in *France*) |
| **ohN** | nasal *on* (as in *bon*) |
| **aN** | nasal *in / ain / ien* (as in *vin*) |
| **uhN** | nasal *un* (as in *un*) |
| **r** | French throaty "r", made at the back of the throat |

Tips: final consonants are usually silent (*parlent* = "parl"). **on** ("we/one") always uses the *il/elle* form.

## 1. être — to be (*eh-truh*)

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| je suis | zhuh swee | I am |
| tu es | tew eh | you are |
| il/elle est | eel/el eh | he/she is |
| nous sommes | noo sum | we are |
| vous êtes | voo zet | you (pl./formal) are |
| ils/elles sont | eel/el sohN | they are |

- Past: **j'ai été** (zhay ay-tay) — I was / have been
- Future: **je serai** (zhuh suh-ray) — I will be
- Sentence: **Je suis fatigué(e) aujourd'hui.** (zhuh swee fah-tee-gay oh-zhoor-dwee) — *I am tired today.*

## Quick grammar notes

- **Regular -er verbs** (parler, aimer, donner, trouver, passer, penser, demander, manger) all follow the same pattern: *-e, -es, -e, -ons, -ez, -ent*. The *je, tu, il, ils* forms sound identical.
- **Liaison:** a normally silent final consonant is pronounced when the next word starts with a vowel: *vous‿avez* (voo zah-vay), *ils‿ont* (eel zohN).
- **Past tense with être:** movement verbs like *aller, venir, partir* use **être** instead of avoir (*je suis allé*), and the past participle agrees with the subject (*elle est allée*).
- **Negation:** wrap the verb with **ne ... pas**: *Je **ne** parle **pas** espagnol.* In casual speech, the *ne* is often dropped.
