# Reflexive (Pronominal) Verbs

Some French verbs come with a small extra pronoun: *je **me** lève* (I get up), *tu **t'**appelles* (you are called), *ils **se** parlent* (they talk to each other). These are **pronominal verbs**, often called **reflexive verbs**. They are everywhere in daily life, especially when you talk about your routine. In six short lessons you will learn the pronouns, twelve everyday verbs, negatives and questions, the past and future, commands, and the verbs whose meaning changes when you add *se*. You already met ten of these verbs in lessons 18–20 of *100 More Common French Verbs*; the forms here match that guide exactly.

## Pronunciation key

| Spelling | Sounds like |
|---|---|
| **zh** | the "s" in *measure* (French **j** / soft **g**) |
| **uh** | the "a" in *about* |
| **ew** | French **u**: say "ee" with your lips rounded like "oo" |
| **N** (capital) | nasal vowel: hum the vowel through your nose, don't say the "n" |
| **ahN** | nasal *an / en* (as in *France*) |
| **ohN** | nasal *on* (as in *bon*) |
| **aN** | nasal *in / ain / ien* (as in *vin*) |
| **uhN** | nasal *un* (as in *un*) |
| **r** | French throaty "r", made at the back of the throat |
| **‿** | liaison: the silent final consonant is pronounced at the start of the next word (*vous‿avez* = voo zah-vay) |

Tips: final consonants are usually silent (*parlent* = "parl"). **on** ("we/one") always uses the *il/elle* form.

## How to use this guide

- Do one lesson at a time, then the matching workbook lesson.
- Read every table aloud, pronoun and all: *je me lève*, not just *lève*. The pronoun is part of the verb.
- Learn the daily-routine verbs as a little story about your own morning.
- Tick the can-do checklist at the end of each lesson before moving on.

## Lesson 1: What pronominal verbs are + the pronouns

### The idea

With a pronominal verb, the action **comes back to the subject**. *Je lave la voiture* = I wash the car. *Je **me** lave* = I wash **myself**. English often drops the "myself" (*I get dressed*, *I sit down*), but French always keeps the pronoun.

In dictionaries and in your verb guides, these verbs are listed with **se** in front: *se lever* (to get up), *s'appeler* (to be called), *s'habiller* (to get dressed).

### The reflexive pronouns

The pronoun always matches the subject, and it goes **in front of the verb**. Before a vowel or a silent *h*, **me, te, se** shorten to **m', t', s'** (this is called *elision*).

| Subject | Pronoun | Before a vowel or silent h | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| je | me (muh) | m' | je me lave / je m'appelle |
| tu | te (tuh) | t' | tu te laves / tu t'habilles |
| il / elle / on | se (suh) | s' | il se lave / il s'amuse |
| nous | nous (noo) | nous | nous nous lavons |
| vous | vous (voo) | vous | vous vous lavez |
| ils / elles | se (suh) | s' | ils se lavent / elles s'habillent |

Yes, with **nous** and **vous** the pronoun is said twice: *nous nous lavons*. Before a vowel, the second one makes a liaison: *nous nous‿habillons* (noo noo zah-bee-yohN).

### s'appeler = to be called

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| je m'appelle | zhuh mah-pel | my name is |
| tu t'appelles | tew tah-pel | your name is |
| il/elle s'appelle | eel/el sah-pel | his/her name is |
| nous nous appelons | noo noo zah-plohN | our names are |
| vous vous appelez | voo voo zah-play | your name is |
| ils/elles s'appellent | eel/el sah-pel | their names are |

- **Je me lave le matin.** (zhuh muh lahv luh mah-taN) — *I wash in the morning.*
- **Je m'appelle Sophie.** (zhuh mah-pel soh-fee) — *My name is Sophie.*
- **Comment tu t'appelles ?** (koh-mahN tew tah-pel) — *What's your name?*
- **Il s'appelle Thomas.** (eel sah-pel toh-mah) — *His name is Thomas.*
- **Tu t'habilles vite.** (tew tah-beey veet) — *You get dressed quickly.*
- **Nous nous habillons pour la fête.** (noo noo zah-bee-yohN poor lah fet) — *We're getting dressed up for the party.*
- **Les enfants se lavent les mains.** (lay zahN-fahN suh lahv lay maN) — *The children wash their hands.*
- **On s'habille et on part !** (ohN sah-beey ay ohN par) — *Let's get dressed and go!*

> **Common mistakes**
> - ✗ *Je lève moi.* → ✓ **Je me lève.** (zhuh muh lev) — the pronoun is me, and it goes before the verb.
> - ✗ *Je me appelle Paul.* → ✓ **Je m'appelle Paul.** (zhuh mah-pel pohl) — me becomes m' before a vowel.
> - ✗ *Nous se levons.* → ✓ **Nous nous levons.** (noo noo luh-vohN) — the pronoun matches the subject: nous … nous.
> - ✗ *Tu se laves.* → ✓ **Tu te laves.** (tew tuh lahv) — tu goes with te.

## Lesson 2: The daily-routine verbs (present tense)

These twelve verbs describe a normal day. Three of them you saw in Lesson 1 (*se laver, s'habiller, s'appeler*). Here are the other nine. Watch the spelling notes: they are the same patterns as in your verb guides.

- **se lever**: *e* → *è* when the ending is silent (*je me lève*, but *nous nous levons*).
- **s'appeler**: double **l** when the ending is silent (*je m'appelle*, but *nous nous appelons*).
- **s'asseoir** is irregular; learn it by heart.
- **se souvenir** conjugates like *venir*, and it is followed by **de**: *je me souviens **de** toi*.

### Body parts take le, la, les

With a reflexive verb, the pronoun already tells us whose body it is, so French uses **le, la, les** (not *mon, ma, mes*) for body parts: *je me brosse **les** dents* = I brush **my** teeth.

- **Je me réveille à 7 h.** (zhuh muh ray-vey ah seh tuhr) — *I wake up at 7 o'clock.*
- **Je me lève tout de suite.** (zhuh muh lev toot sweet) — *I get up straight away.*
- **Je me brosse les dents.** (zhuh muh brohs lay dahN) — *I brush my teeth.*
- **Je me lave les mains.** (zhuh muh lahv lay maN) — *I wash my hands.*
- **Elle se brosse les cheveux.** (el suh brohs lay shuh-vuh) — *She brushes her hair.*
- **Tu te dépêches ? Le bus arrive !** (tew tuh day-pesh ? luh bews ah-reev) — *Are you hurrying? The bus is coming!*
- **Nous nous asseyons près de la fenêtre.** (noo noo zah-seh-yohN preh duh lah fuh-neh-truh) — *We sit down near the window.*
- **Je me souviens de toi.** (zhuh muh soo-vyaN duh twah) — *I remember you.*
- **Les enfants s'amusent dans le jardin.** (lay zahN-fahN sah-mewz dahN luh zhar-daN) — *The children are having fun in the garden.*
- **Je m'occupe de mon chat.** (zhuh moh-kewp duh mohN shah) — *I look after my cat.*
- **Ils se couchent à 22 h.** (eel suh koosh ah vaNt-duh zuhr) — *They go to bed at 10 p.m.*

> **Common mistakes**
> - ✗ *Je me brosse mes dents.* → ✓ **Je me brosse les dents.** (zhuh muh brohs lay dahN) — body parts take les after a reflexive verb.
> - ✗ *Je me souviens ce film.* → ✓ **Je me souviens de ce film.** (zhuh muh soo-vyaN duh suh feelm) — se souvenir is followed by de.
> - ✗ *Il s'occupe son fils.* → ✓ **Il s'occupe de son fils.** (eel soh-kewp duh sohN fees) — s'occuper is followed by de.
> - ✗ *Nous nous lèvons.* → ✓ **Nous nous levons.** (noo noo luh-vohN) — no è when the ending is said aloud.

## Lesson 3: Negatives and questions

### Negation: ne + pronoun + verb + pas

The reflexive pronoun stays **glued to the front of the verb**. *Ne* goes in front of that pronoun-verb block and *pas* after it: *je **ne** me lève **pas*** (I don't get up). Here *ne* never shortens, because the word after it is the pronoun (*me, te, se, nous, vous*): *je **ne** m'habille pas*.

| Affirmative | Negative | Pronunciation |
|---|---|---|
| je me lève | je ne me lève pas | zhuh nuh muh lev pah |
| tu t'habilles | tu ne t'habilles pas | tew nuh tah-beey pah |
| il se dépêche | il ne se dépêche pas | eel nuh suh day-pesh pah |
| nous nous couchons | nous ne nous couchons pas | noo nuh noo koo-shohN pah |
| vous vous amusez | vous ne vous amusez pas | voo nuh voo zah-mew-zay pah |
| elles se souviennent | elles ne se souviennent pas | el nuh suh soo-vyen pah |

- **Je ne me lève pas tôt.** (zhuh nuh muh lev pah toh) — *I don't get up early.*
- **Il ne s'habille pas.** (eel nuh sah-beey pah) — *He isn't getting dressed.*
- **Nous ne nous dépêchons pas.** (noo nuh noo day-peh-shohN pah) — *We aren't hurrying.*
- **Elle ne se souvient pas de moi.** (el nuh suh soo-vyaN pah duh mwah) — *She doesn't remember me.*
- **Je ne m'amuse pas.** (zhuh nuh mah-mewz pah) — *I'm not having fun.*

### Questions

All three question styles from *Asking Questions* work. The reflexive pronoun **always stays in front of the verb**, even in inversion: *Te lèves-tu ?*

| Casual | Standard | Formal |
|---|---|---|
| Tu te lèves tôt ? | Est-ce que tu te lèves tôt ? | Te lèves-tu tôt ? |
| Vous vous appelez comment ? | Comment est-ce que vous vous appelez ? | Comment vous appelez-vous ? |
| Tu te couches à quelle heure ? | À quelle heure est-ce que tu te couches ? | À quelle heure te couches-tu ? |

- **Tu te lèves tôt ?** (tew tuh lev toh) — *Do you get up early?*
- **Est-ce que tu te lèves tôt ?** (es-kuh tew tuh lev toh) — *Do you get up early?*
- **Te lèves-tu tôt ?** (tuh lev-tew toh) — *Do you get up early? (formal)*
- **Comment vous appelez-vous ?** (koh-mahN voo zah-play-voo) — *What is your name? (polite)*
- **À quelle heure te couches-tu ?** (ah kel uhr tuh koosh-tew) — *What time do you go to bed?*
- **Se lève-t-elle tôt ?** (suh lev-tel toh) — *Does she get up early?*
- **Vous vous souvenez de lui ?** (voo voo soo-vuh-nay duh lwee) — *Do you remember him?*
- **Ne te lèves-tu pas ?** (nuh tuh lev-tew pah) — *Aren't you getting up? (formal, rare)*

> **Common mistakes**
> - ✗ *Je me ne lève pas.* → ✓ **Je ne me lève pas.** (zhuh nuh muh lev pah) — ne comes before the pronoun.
> - ✗ *Je ne lève me pas.* → ✓ **Je ne me lève pas.** (zhuh nuh muh lev pah) — the pronoun stays glued to the front of the verb.
> - ✗ *Lèves-tu te tôt ?* → ✓ **Te lèves-tu tôt ?** (tuh lev-tew toh) — in inversion, the pronoun still comes first.
> - ✗ *Est-ce que te lèves-tu tôt ?* → ✓ **Est-ce que tu te lèves tôt ?** (es-kuh tew tuh lev toh) — est-ce que + normal order.

### Can-do checklist

- [ ] I can make a reflexive verb negative (je ne me lève pas).
- [ ] I can ask a question with a reflexive verb in three styles.
- [ ] I can ask someone what time they get up and go to bed.

## Lesson 4: Past and future

### Passé composé: always with être

Every pronominal verb forms its passé composé with **être** (not avoir): pronoun + être + past participle. Because the helper is *être*, the participle **agrees with the subject**: add **-e** for a woman, **-s** for a group, **-es** for a group of women. *Je me suis levé* (a man), *je me suis levée* (a woman), *elle s'est levée*. The endings are silent: all of these sound the same.

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| je me suis levé(e) | zhuh muh swee luh-vay | I got up |
| tu t'es levé(e) | tew teh luh-vay | you got up |
| il s'est levé / elle s'est levée | eel seh luh-vay / el seh luh-vay | he / she got up |
| nous nous sommes levé(e)s | noo noo sum luh-vay | we got up |
| vous vous êtes levé(e)(s) | voo voo zet luh-vay | you got up |
| ils se sont levés / elles se sont levées | eel suh sohN luh-vay / el suh sohN luh-vay | they got up |

- **Elle s'est levée à 7 h.** (el seh luh-vay ah seh tuhr) — *She got up at 7 o'clock.*
- **Je me suis réveillé tard.** (zhuh muh swee ray-veh-yay tar) — *I woke up late. (a man speaking)*
- **Je me suis réveillée tard.** (zhuh muh swee ray-veh-yay tar) — *I woke up late. (a woman speaking)*
- **Ils se sont couchés à minuit.** (eel suh sohN koo-shay ah mee-nwee) — *They went to bed at midnight.*
- **Nous nous sommes bien amusés.** (noo noo sum byaN nah-mew-zay) — *We had a great time.*
- **Marie et Léa se sont dépêchées.** (mah-ree ay lay-ah suh sohN day-peh-shay) — *Marie and Léa hurried.*
- **Tu t'es habillée vite !** (tew teh zah-bee-yay veet) — *You got dressed quickly! (to a woman)*
- **Je ne me suis pas réveillé.** (zhuh nuh muh swee pah ray-veh-yay) — *I didn't wake up. (ne … pas around the pronoun + être)*

A light note: when a **body part or other object** follows the verb, the participle does **not** agree: *Elle s'est lavée* (she washed), but *Elle s'est lavé **les mains*** (she washed her hands).

- **Elle s'est lavé les mains.** (el seh lah-vay lay maN) — *She washed her hands.*
- **Elle s'est brossé les dents.** (el seh broh-say lay dahN) — *She brushed her teeth.*

> **Teacher's note:** for "each other" verbs in the past (*ils se sont parlé*), the agreement rules get tricky. At this level, use them in the present only.

### Futur proche: aller + the reflexive infinitive

The pronoun in front of the infinitive must **match the subject**: *je vais **me** lever*, *nous allons **nous** coucher*. Never *je vais se lever*.

| French | Pronunciation | English |
|---|---|---|
| je vais me lever | zhuh veh muh luh-vay | I'm going to get up |
| tu vas te lever | tew vah tuh luh-vay | you're going to get up |
| il/elle va se lever | eel/el vah suh luh-vay | he/she is going to get up |
| nous allons nous lever | noo zah-lohN noo luh-vay | we're going to get up |
| vous allez vous lever | voo zah-lay voo luh-vay | you're going to get up |
| ils/elles vont se lever | eel/el vohN suh luh-vay | they're going to get up |

- **Je vais me lever.** (zhuh veh muh luh-vay) — *I'm going to get up.*
- **Nous allons nous coucher.** (noo zah-lohN noo koo-shay) — *We're going to go to bed.*
- **Tu vas t'habiller ?** (tew vah tah-bee-yay) — *Are you going to get dressed?*
- **Ils vont s'amuser.** (eel vohN sah-mew-zay) — *They're going to have fun.*
- **Je ne vais pas me dépêcher.** (zhuh nuh veh pah muh day-peh-shay) — *I'm not going to hurry.*

### Futur simple

The pronoun stays in front, and the future endings are the usual ones (*-ai, -as, -a, -ons, -ez, -ont*).

- **Je me lèverai tôt demain.** (zhuh muh lev-ray toh duh-maN) — *I will get up early tomorrow.*
- **Tu te coucheras tard.** (tew tuh koosh-rah tar) — *You will go to bed late.*
- **Il s'habillera vite.** (eel sah-beey-rah veet) — *He will get dressed quickly.*
- **Je me souviendrai de toi.** (zhuh muh soo-vyaN-dray duh twah) — *I will remember you.*
- **Nous nous souviendrons de ce voyage.** (noo noo soo-vyaN-drohN duh suh vwah-yahzh) — *We will remember this trip.*

> **Common mistakes**
> - ✗ *Il s'a couché.* → ✓ **Il s'est couché.** (eel seh koo-shay) — pronominal verbs always use être.
> - ✗ *Elle s'est levé tôt.* → ✓ **Elle s'est levée tôt.** (el seh luh-vay toh) — the participle agrees with elle.
> - ✗ *Je me suis lavée les mains.* (a woman) → ✓ **Je me suis lavé les mains.** (zhuh muh swee lah-vay lay maN) — no agreement when a body part follows.
> - ✗ *Je vais se coucher.* → ✓ **Je vais me coucher.** (zhuh veh muh koo-shay) — the pronoun matches je.

### Can-do checklist

- [ ] I can say what I did this morning with the passé composé and être.
- [ ] I can make the participle agree (elle s'est levée, ils se sont couchés).
- [ ] I can talk about plans with the futur proche (je vais me lever).
- [ ] I can use a few verbs in the futur simple (je me lèverai).

## Lesson 5: The imperative (commands)

### Affirmative: verb-hyphen-pronoun

In a positive command, the pronoun moves **after** the verb with a **hyphen**, and **te** becomes **toi**. For *-er* verbs, the *tu* form drops its **-s**: *Lève-toi !* (not *Lèves-toi*).

| Infinitive | Affirmative (tu) | Affirmative (vous) | Affirmative (nous) | Negative (tu) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| se lever | Lève-toi ! | Levez-vous ! | Levons-nous ! | Ne te lève pas ! |
| se dépêcher | Dépêche-toi ! | Dépêchez-vous ! | Dépêchons-nous ! | Ne te dépêche pas ! |
| s'asseoir | Assieds-toi ! | Asseyez-vous ! | Asseyons-nous ! | Ne t'assieds pas ! |
| s'habiller | Habille-toi ! | Habillez-vous ! | Habillons-nous ! | Ne t'habille pas ! |
| se coucher | Couche-toi ! | Couchez-vous ! | Couchons-nous ! | Ne te couche pas ! |

- **Lève-toi !** (lev twah) — *Get up!*
- **Levez-vous !** (luh-vay voo) — *Get up! (to several people / polite)*
- **Levons-nous !** (luh-vohN noo) — *Let's get up!*
- **Dépêche-toi !** (day-pesh twah) — *Hurry up!*
- **Assieds-toi !** (ah-syay twah) — *Sit down!*
- **Asseyez-vous, s'il vous plaît.** (ah-seh-yay voo, seel voo pleh) — *Please sit down.*
- **Amuse-toi bien !** (ah-mewz twah byaN) — *Have fun!*
- **Souviens-toi !** (soo-vyaN twah) — *Remember!*

### Negative: ne + pronoun + verb + pas

In a negative command, everything goes back to the normal order: the pronoun stays **in front** of the verb, and **te** stays **te**.

- **Ne te lève pas !** (nuh tuh lev pah) — *Don't get up!*
- **Ne vous dépêchez pas !** (nuh voo day-peh-shay pah) — *Don't hurry!*
- **Ne t'assieds pas ici !** (nuh tah-syay pah ee-see) — *Don't sit here!*
- **Ne t'inquiète pas !** (nuh taN-kyet pah) — *Don't worry! (s'inquiéter = to worry)*
- **Ne nous couchons pas trop tard !** (nuh noo koo-shohN pah troh tar) — *Let's not go to bed too late!*

> **Common mistakes**
> - ✗ *Ne lève-toi pas !* → ✓ **Ne te lève pas !** (nuh tuh lev pah) — negative commands use normal order with te.
> - ✗ *Lèves-toi !* → ✓ **Lève-toi !** (lev twah) — the tu command of -er verbs drops the s.
> - ✗ *Lève-te !* → ✓ **Lève-toi !** (lev twah) — after the verb, te becomes toi.
> - ✗ *Te lève !* → ✓ **Lève-toi !** (lev twah) — in a positive command, the pronoun goes after the verb.

### Can-do checklist

- [ ] I can give positive commands with toi, vous and nous (Lève-toi !).
- [ ] I can give negative commands (Ne te lève pas !).
- [ ] I can tell a friend to hurry up, sit down and have fun.

## Lesson 6: "Each other" + reflexive or not?

### Reciprocal verbs: each other

With a plural subject (*nous, vous, ils, elles, on*), the pronoun can mean **each other**: *Ils se parlent* = they talk to each other. Any verb that can take a person as its object can do this.

- **Ils se parlent tous les jours.** (eel suh parl too lay zhoor) — *They talk to each other every day.*
- **Nous nous téléphonons le soir.** (noo noo tay-lay-foh-nohN luh swahr) — *We phone each other in the evening. (téléphoner = to phone)*
- **On se voit demain ?** (ohN suh vwah duh-maN) — *Shall we see each other tomorrow?*
- **Elles s'aiment beaucoup.** (el sem boh-koo) — *They love each other a lot.*
- **Vous vous écrivez ?** (voo voo zay-kree-vay) — *Do you write to each other?*
- **Paul et Marie se regardent.** (pohl ay mah-ree suh ruh-gard) — *Paul and Marie look at each other.*
- **On se retrouve au café ?** (ohN suh ruh-troov oh kah-fay) — *Shall we meet at the café?*

### Reflexive or not? The meaning can change

Many verbs exist **with and without** *se*. Without *se*, you do the action **to something or someone else**. With *se*, it comes back to you, and sometimes the meaning shifts a lot.

| Non-reflexive | Meaning | Reflexive | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| laver | to wash (something) | se laver | to wash (oneself) |
| appeler | to call | s'appeler | to be called |
| ennuyer | to bore | s'ennuyer | to be bored |
| lever | to raise, to lift | se lever | to get up |
| coucher | to put to bed | se coucher | to go to bed |
| promener | to walk (the dog) | se promener | to go for a walk |
| arrêter | to stop (something) | s'arrêter | to stop (oneself), to come to a stop |
| occuper | to occupy | s'occuper de | to take care of |

- **Je lave la voiture.** (zhuh lahv lah vwah-tewr) — *I wash the car.*
- **Je me lave.** (zhuh muh lahv) — *I wash (myself).*
- **J'appelle mon frère.** (zhah-pel mohN frair) — *I call my brother.*
- **Je m'appelle Sophie.** (zhuh mah-pel soh-fee) — *My name is Sophie.*
- **Ce film m'ennuie.** (suh feelm mahN-nwee) — *This film bores me.*
- **Je m'ennuie.** (zhuh mahN-nwee) — *I'm bored.*
- **Je promène le chien.** (zhuh proh-men luh shyaN) — *I walk the dog.*
- **Je me promène au parc.** (zhuh muh proh-men oh park) — *I go for a walk in the park.*
- **Je couche les enfants à 20 h.** (zhuh koosh lay zahN-fahN ah vaN tuhr) — *I put the children to bed at 8 p.m.*
- **Le bus s'arrête ici.** (luh bews sah-ret ee-see) — *The bus stops here.*

### Recap: one morning, two tenses

**Today (present)**, Léa describes her routine:

- **Je me réveille à 6 h 30.** (zhuh muh ray-vey ah see zuhr trahNt) — *I wake up at 6:30.*
- **Je me lève tout de suite.** (zhuh muh lev toot sweet) — *I get up straight away.*
- **Je me lave et je me brosse les dents.** (zhuh muh lahv ay zhuh muh brohs lay dahN) — *I wash and brush my teeth.*
- **Je m'habille et je me dépêche.** (zhuh mah-beey ay zhuh muh day-pesh) — *I get dressed and I hurry.*
- **Le soir, je m'occupe de mon chat et je me couche à 23 h.** (luh swahr, zhuh moh-kewp duh mohN shah ay zhuh muh koosh ah vaNt-trwah zuhr) — *In the evening, I look after my cat and go to bed at 11 p.m.*

**Yesterday (passé composé)**, the same day told by Léa (so the participles take **-e**, except before *les dents*):

- **Hier, je me suis réveillée à 6 h 30.** (yair, zhuh muh swee ray-veh-yay ah see zuhr trahNt) — *Yesterday I woke up at 6:30.*
- **Je me suis levée tout de suite.** (zhuh muh swee luh-vay toot sweet) — *I got up straight away.*
- **Je me suis lavée et je me suis brossé les dents.** (zhuh muh swee lah-vay ay zhuh muh swee broh-say lay dahN) — *I washed and brushed my teeth.*
- **Je me suis habillée et je me suis dépêchée.** (zhuh muh swee zah-bee-yay ay zhuh muh swee day-peh-shay) — *I got dressed and I hurried.*
- **Je me suis couchée à 23 h.** (zhuh muh swee koo-shay ah vaNt-trwah zuhr) — *I went to bed at 11 p.m.*

> **Common mistakes**
> - ✗ *Je m'appelle est Paul.* → ✓ **Je m'appelle Paul.** (zhuh mah-pel pohl) — s'appeler already contains the verb; no est.
> - ✗ *Ils parlent se.* → ✓ **Ils se parlent.** (eel suh parl) — the pronoun goes before the verb.
> - ✗ *Je promène au parc.* (meaning "I go for a walk") → ✓ **Je me promène au parc.** (zhuh muh proh-men oh park) — without se, promener needs an object (le chien).
> - ✗ *Je m'appelle mon frère.* (meaning "I call my brother") → ✓ **J'appelle mon frère.** (zhah-pel mohN frair) — to call someone is appeler, without se.

### Can-do checklist

- [ ] I can say that people do things to each other (ils se parlent).
- [ ] I can tell laver / se laver, appeler / s'appeler and ennuyer / s'ennuyer apart.
- [ ] I can tell the story of my morning in the present and in the passé composé.
